# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to **NoChipsPoker** and all other iOS apps published by **simplyCreative** (including any future apps). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly how we handle data in NoChipsPoker — in plain English and with full transparency.

### 1. We Collect No Data That Leaves Your Device
NoChipsPoker is **100% offline**. There are no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

The only information that exists is stored **locally on your iPhone or iPad**:

- Saved poker games and chip counts (stored as simple JSON files in the app’s Documents folder)
- App settings and preferences (also stored as JSON)
- One custom background photo (if you choose to pick one from your photo library using Apple’s PhotosPicker — saved locally as a JPEG)
- Your optional admin PIN (stored securely in the iOS Keychain using Apple’s Security framework)
- A simple “has seen onboarding” flag (stored in UserDefaults)

**Nothing** is ever sent to us, to Apple (except the purchase itself), or to any other company.

### 2. Photo Library Access
The app uses Apple’s built-in `PhotosUI` picker **only** if you want to set a custom background image.  
- The photo stays entirely on your device.  
- It is never uploaded, shared, analyzed, or transmitted.  
- You can delete it at any time by clearing the background in the app or uninstalling NoChipsPoker.

No `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` string is required because we use the modern PhotosPicker API (iOS 16+).

### 3. In-App Purchases
Premium features are unlocked through a **one-time non-consumable purchase** handled entirely by Apple’s StoreKit.  
We never see your payment information, credit card, or any purchase details.

### 4. No User Accounts or Login
There is no login, no email, no Apple Sign In, and no account system. The only “authentication” is an optional local admin PIN that you set yourself and that never leaves your device.

### 5. Sound, Haptics & Other Features
- Sound effects are generated and played locally using `AVFoundation`.  
- Haptic feedback uses Apple’s built-in `UIKit` haptics.  
- No audio is recorded. No microphone or camera is ever used.

### 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We **do not** share, sell, rent, or disclose any data to anyone — because no data ever leaves your device.

### 7. Your Rights & Data Deletion
You have full control:
- Delete any saved games or the background photo inside the app.
- Uninstall NoChipsPoker to remove everything instantly.
- Since we hold zero data on our side, there is nothing for us to delete upon request.

### 8. Children’s Privacy
NoChipsPoker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

### 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we ever make a material change, we will update this page and note the new “Last updated” date. Because the app is offline, the best way to see the latest version is to check this page in your browser or inside the app’s Support Hub.

If you have any questions about this policy or the app’s privacy practices, please reach out:
