Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

At Pause & Pray, we believe your relationship with God is deeply personal and sacred. We created this app not to collect data, but to gently encourage you to pause, pray, and approach your phone with greater intention.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide a meaningful prayer-before-app experience.

  • Family Controls & Shield Data — Anonymous tokens from Apple’s Family Controls. We never see which apps you use or how often.
  • Prayer History — Stored only on your device using SwiftData. Nothing is sent to our servers unless you export a prayer.
  • AI Prayer Requests — Minimal, anonymized prompts sent securely to our backend.

We do not collect your name, email, location, actual app usage, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Everything serves one purpose: helping you draw closer to God before opening apps.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data stays on your iPhone. AI requests travel over encrypted connections. We do not store generated prayers on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Limited sharing occurs only with Apple (Family Controls) and our secure backend.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can remove shields, delete your prayer history, or delete the app at any time.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new date.
