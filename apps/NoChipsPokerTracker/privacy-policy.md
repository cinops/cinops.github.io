# NoChipsPoker Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to **NoChipsPoker** (published by simplyCreative). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

### 1. We Collect No Data That Leaves Your Device
NoChipsPoker is **100% offline**. There are no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

All data stays **locally on your device**:
- Saved games and chip counts (JSON in app’s Documents folder)
- App settings (JSON)
- Optional custom background photo (JPEG saved locally)
- Optional admin PIN (stored in iOS Keychain)
- Onboarding flag (UserDefaults)

Nothing is ever sent to us or anyone else.

### 2. Photo Library Access
Used only via Apple’s `PhotosUI` picker to set a custom background image. The photo never leaves your device.

### 3. In-App Purchases
Handled 100% by Apple’s StoreKit. We see nothing.

### 4. No Accounts or Login
Only a local optional admin PIN.

### 5. Sound, Haptics & Other Features
All processed on-device. No recording, no camera, no microphone, no location, etc.

### 6. Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, or transmit any data.

### 7. Your Rights
Delete games/photos inside the app or uninstall to remove everything.

### 8. Children’s Privacy
Not directed at children under 13.

### 9. Changes to This Policy
We will update this page and the date above if needed.
